### [DEP] Depression
* **Core Presenting Complaints:** Persistent sadness, depressed mood, anxiety, or loss of interest/pleasure in activities that are normally pleasurable.
* **Secondary/Associated Symptoms:** Multiple persistent physical symptoms with no clear cause, low energy, fatigue, and sleep problems.
* **Triage Rule:** "Trigger this module if the patient presents with persistent sadness, anhedonia, unexplained fatigue, or multiple persistent physical complaints with no clear medical cause."

### [PSY] Psychoses
* **Core Presenting Complaints:** Hearing voices or seeing things that are not there (hallucinations), fixed false beliefs not shared by others (delusions), and marked behavioural changes (neglecting usual responsibilities).
* **Secondary/Associated Symptoms:** Agitated or aggressive behaviour, decreased or increased activity, and lack of realization that they are having mental health problems. 
* **Triage Rule:** "Trigger this module if the patient presents with severe behavioural changes, hallucinations, delusions, or unexplained agitated/aggressive behaviour."

### [EPI] Epilepsy / Seizures
* **Core Presenting Complaints:** Convulsive movements, fits, or seizures (acute or continuous) accompanied by loss of or impaired consciousness.
* **Secondary/Associated Symptoms:** *During convulsion:* stiffness, rigidity, tongue bite, physical injury, or incontinence. *After convulsion:* fatigue, drowsiness, sleepiness, confusion, abnormal behaviour, headache, muscle aches, or unilateral weakness.
* **Triage Rule:** "Trigger this module if the patient presents with active convulsions, continuous convulsions (Status Epilepticus), or a history of seizures with post-ictal symptoms."

### [CMH] Child & Adolescent Mental & Behavioural Disorders
* **Core Presenting Complaints:** Problems with development, emotions, or behaviour (e.g., inattention, over-activity, repeated defiance/disobedience, physical aggression, or rule-breaking).
* **Secondary/Associated Symptoms:** Difficulty keeping up with peers, severe/frequent tantrums, excessive isolation, school refusal, being easily distracted or disruptive. Associated risk factors include malnutrition, abuse/neglect, or chronic diseases.
* **Triage Rule:** "Trigger this module if a child or adolescent presents with developmental, emotional, or behavioural difficulties reported by the patient, carers, teachers, or community workers."

### [DEM] Dementia
* **Core Presenting Complaints:** Decline or problems with memory (severe forgetfulness), orientation issues (awareness of time, place, and person), and difficulties carrying out usual domestic/social activities.
* **Secondary/Associated Symptoms:** Mood or behavioural problems (apathy, appearing uninterested), loss of emotional control (easily upset, irritable, or tearful), and agitated or aggressive behaviour.
* **Triage Rule:** "Trigger this module if the patient presents with progressive memory decline, disorientation, and functional impairment in daily activities."

### [SUB] Disorders due to Substance Use
* **Core Presenting Complaints:** Appearing affected by substances (smell of alcohol, slurred speech, sedation, erratic behaviour), symptoms of acute intoxication/overdose (pinpoint or dilated pupils, racing thoughts, unresponsiveness), or withdrawal (tremors, sweating, continuous convulsions, delirium).
* **Secondary/Associated Symptoms:** Deterioration of social functioning, unkempt appearance, balance/walking problems (nystagmus), recurrent requests for psychoactive medications, IV drug-related infections, and signs of chronic liver disease (jaundice, ascites, spider naevi). Incidental lab findings include macrocytic anaemia and elevated MCV.
* **Triage Rule:** "Trigger this module if the patient presents with signs of acute intoxication, overdose, withdrawal, chronic physiological manifestations of substance abuse, or drug-seeking behaviour."

### [SUI] Self-harm / Suicide
* **Core Presenting Complaints:** Current thoughts, plans, or acts of self-harm/suicide, a history of such acts, or emergency presentations involving poisoning, intoxication, or bleeding from self-inflicted wounds.
* **Secondary/Associated Symptoms:** Extreme hopelessness and despair, extreme emotional distress, extreme agitation/violence linked to suicidal distress, or the presence of chronic pain and other priority conditions.
* **Triage Rule:** "Trigger this module if the patient presents with any thoughts, plans, history, or active attempts of suicide/self-harm, or expresses extreme despair and hopelessness."
