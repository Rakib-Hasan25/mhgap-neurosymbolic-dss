ASSESS FOR IMPROVEMENT

[Box] Is the person improving?

Decision flow:
[Is the person improving?] -> YES
[Is the person improving?] -> NO

YES branch:
[YES] -> 
- Continue with treatment plan
- Follow-up as needed

NO branch:
[NO] -> 
If the person is not improving or the person or the carer insists on further investigations and treatment:
- Review Protocols 1 and 2
- Consider consulting a specialist.

Right-side box:
RECOMMENDATIONS ON FREQUENCY OF CONTACT
» Ask the person to return in 2-4 weeks if their symptoms do not improve or if, at any time, their symptoms worsen.
