**[Decision Box 2]**
Has the person been symptom free for 9-12 months?

--> **[Path: NO]** --> Leads to Action Box 3 (Left)
--> **[Path: YES]** --> Leads to Action Box 4 (Right)

***

**[Action Box 3]** *(From NO path)*
» Continue medication until person is symptom free for 9-12 months.

**[Action Box 4]** *(From YES path)*
» Discuss with the person the risks and benefits of stopping the medication
» Taper the dose of medication gradually, over a minimum of 4 weeks. Monitor the person for symptom recurrence.
