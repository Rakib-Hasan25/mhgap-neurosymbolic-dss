**[Box 1]**
IS THERE A HISTORY OF MANIA?

Have several of the following symptoms occurred simultaneously, lasting for at least 1 week, and severely enough to interfere significantly with work and social activities or requiring hospitalization or confinement?

– Elevation of mood and/or irritability
– Decreased need for sleep
– Increased activity, feeling of increased energy, increased talkativeness or rapid speech
– Impulsive or reckless behaviours such as excessive spending, making important decisions without planning and sexual indiscretion
– Loss of normal social inhibitions resulting in inappropriate behaviours
– Being easily distracted
– Unrealistically inflated self-esteem
