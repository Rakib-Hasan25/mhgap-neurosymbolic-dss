• Extreme hopelessness and despair, current thoughts/plan/act of self-harm suicide or history thereof, act of self-harm with signs of poisoning/intoxication, bleeding from self-inflicted wound, loss of consciousness and/or extreme lethargy. OR
• Any of the priority MNS conditions, chronic pain or extreme emotional distress
