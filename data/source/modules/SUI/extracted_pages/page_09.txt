SUI 3 » Follow-up

[1]
ASSESS FOR IMPROVEMENT

[Decision Box]
Is the person improving?

Decision paths:
[Is the person improving?] -> YES
[Is the person improving?] -> NO

YES path:
[YES] -> 
- Decrease contact as the person improves.
- Continue following-up for 2 years, further decreasing contact according to improvement (e.g. once every 2-4 weeks after the initial 2 months, and twice in the second year).

NO path:
[NO] ->
- Increase intensity or duration of contact as necessary.
- Refer to specialist as needed.

Both paths converge to:

[2]
ROUTINELY ASSESS FOR THOUGHTS AND PLANS OF SELF-HARM/SUICIDE

- At every contact, routinely assess for suicidal thoughts and plans.
- Is the risk of self-harm/suicide imminent? See SUI 1 (Assessment).

Right-side box:
RECOMMENDATIONS ON FREQUENCY OF CONTACT

- MAINTAIN REGULAR CONTACT (via telephone, home visits, letters or contact cards) more frequently initially (e.g. daily, weekly) for the first 2 months.
- Follow-up for as long as the risk of self-harm/suicide persists.
