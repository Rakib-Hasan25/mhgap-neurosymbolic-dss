**[Care Actions Box]**
» Supportive care.  
» Monitor vital signs.  
» Lay the person on their side to prevent aspiration.  
» Give oxygen if available.  
» Consider intravenous (i.v.), rehydration but do not give fluids orally while sedated.  
» Observe the person until fully recovered or transported to hospital.
