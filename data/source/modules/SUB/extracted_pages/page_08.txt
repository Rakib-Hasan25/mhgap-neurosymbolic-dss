ASSESS AND MANAGE A – C

[A]
Person has stopped drinking in the last week: confusion, hallucination, racing thoughts, anxiety, agitation, disorientation, typically in association with either stimulant intoxication or alcohol (or other sedative) withdrawal.

→ [Box]
Suspect  
ALCOHOL OR BENZODIAZEPINE WITHDRAWAL DELIRIUM

→ [Box]
» If the person is showing other signs of alcohol withdrawal (tremors, sweating, vital signs changes)  
– Treat with diazepam 10–20 mg p.o.  
– TRANSFER TO HOSPITAL. +

» Manage delirium with antipsychotics such as haloperidol 1–2.5 mg p.o. or i.m.
