**TABLE 1: Antiepileptic medications (cont.)**

| MEDICATION | ORAL DOSING | SIDE EFFECTS | CONTRAINDICATIONS / CAUTIONS |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PHENOBARBITAL** | **Adults:**<br>Start 60 mg daily in 1-2 divided doses.<br>Increase weekly by 2.5-5 mg (maximum 180 mg daily).<br><br>**Children:**<br>Start 2-3 mg/kg daily in 2 divided doses.<br>Increase weekly by 1-2 mg/kg daily depending on tolerance (maximum 6mg daily). | **Common:** Sedation, hyperactivity in children, ataxia, nystagmus, sexual dysfunction, depression.<br><br>**Serious:** Liver failure (hypersensitivity reaction), decreased bone mineral density. | Contraindicated in patients with acute intermittent porphyria.<br><br>Lower doses for patients with kidney or liver disease. |
| **PHENYTOIN** | **Adults:**<br>Start 150-200 mg daily in two divided doses.<br>Increase by 50 mg daily every 3-4 weeks (max 400 mg daily).<br><br>**Children:**<br>Start 3-4 mg/kg daily in 2 divided doses.<br>Increase by 5 mg/kg daily every 3-4 weeks (maximum 300 mg per day).<br><br>🚼 **Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding:** Avoid<br><br>🧓 **Older adults:** Use lower doses | **Common:** Sedation, confusion, dizziness, tremor, motor twitching, ataxia, double vision, nystagmus, slurred speech, nausea, vomiting, constipation.<br><br>**Serious:** Hematologic abnormalities, hepatitis, polyneuropathy, gum hypertrophy, acne, lymphadenopathy, increase in suicidal ideation. | Lower doses for patients with kidney or liver disease. |
| **SODIUM VALPROATE** | **Adults:**<br>Start 400 mg daily in 2 divided doses.<br>Increase by 500 mg daily each week (maximum 3000 mg daily).<br><br>**Children:**<br>Start 15-20 mg/kg daily in 2-3 divided doses.<br>Increase each week by 15 mg/kg daily (max 15-40 mg/kg daily).<br><br>🚼 **Women who are pregnant:** Avoid<br><br>🧓 **Older adults:** Use lower doses | **Common:** Sedation, headache, tremor, ataxia, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, weight gain, transient hair loss.<br><br>**Serious:** Impaired hepatic function, thrombocytopenia, leukopenia, drowsiness/confusion (valproate-induced hyperammonemic encephalopathy, a sign of toxicity), liver failure, hemorrhagic pancreatitis. | Use with caution if underlying or suspected hepatic disease.<br><br>Drug-drug interactions: Valproate levels decreased by carbamazepine, increased by aspirin. |
