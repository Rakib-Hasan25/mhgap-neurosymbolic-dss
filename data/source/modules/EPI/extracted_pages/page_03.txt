[Action Box 2]
A large box containing two columns of instructions. A red exclamation mark icon is on the right border of this box.

[Left Column]
*   » Check AIRWAY, BREATHING, CIRCULATION (ABCs)
    Ensure the person has nothing in their airway, is breathing well and has a stable pulse
*   » Check BLOOD PRESSURE, TEMPERATURE and RESPIRATORY RATE
*   » Start timing the duration of the convulsions, if possible
*   » Make sure the person is in a safe place and if possible, put them down on their side to help breathing; loosen any neckties or clothing around the neck, take off eye glasses, and place something soft under the head (if available)

[Right Column]
*   » Place an intravenous (i.v.) line for medication/ fluid administration if possible
*   » ⊗ DO NOT LEAVE THE PERSON ALONE
*   » ⊗ DO NOT PUT ANYTHING IN THE MOUTH
*   » FOR A PERSON WITH POSSIBLE HEAD INJURY, NEUROINFECTION (FEVER) OR FOCAL DEFICITS, REFER URGENTLY TO HOSPITAL ✚
