# MODULE: DEPRESSION

## 1. VARIABLE DICTIONARY

- **Core Symptoms:** 
  - Persistent depressed mood
  - Markedly diminished interest in or pleasure from activities
- **Secondary / Additional Symptoms:** 
  - Disturbed sleep or sleeping too much
  - Indecisiveness
  - Significant change in appetite or weight (decrease or increase)
  - Observable agitation or physical restlessness
  - Beliefs of worthlessness or excessive guilt
  - Talking or moving more slowly than usual
  - Fatigue or loss of energy
  - Hopelessness
  - Reduced concentration
  - Suicidal thoughts or acts
  - Psychotic symptoms (delusions or hallucinations)
- **Physical/Differential Symptoms:**
  - Signs of anaemia, malnutrition, hypothyroidism
  - Mood changes from substance use or medication (e.g., steroids)
- **Mania Symptoms (Bipolar Rule-Out):**
  - Elevation of mood and/or irritability
  - Decreased need for sleep
  - Increased activity, feeling of increased energy, increased talkativeness, rapid speech
  - Impulsive or reckless behaviors (excessive spending, sexual indiscretion, etc.)
  - Loss of normal social inhibitions resulting in inappropriate behavior
  - Being easily distracted
  - Unrealistically inflated self-esteem
- **Duration Thresholds:** 
  - `>= 2 weeks` (Required for core and additional depression symptoms)
  - `>= 1 week` (Required for mania symptoms)
  - `< 6 months` (Timeframe for a recent major loss/bereavement)
  - `9 - 12 months` (Required symptom-free duration for maintenance phase)
  - `>= 4 weeks` (Minimum timeframe for medication tapering)
- **Demographic / Special Flags:** 
  - Child / Adolescent (< 12 years of age)
  - Adolescent (12 years of age or older)
  - Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding
  - Woman of childbearing age considering pregnancy
  - Older Adults
  - People with cardiovascular disease
  - People with history of seizure, hyperthyroidism, urinary retention, or glaucoma
  - Imminent risk of suicide or self-harm

## 2. RED FLAGS & REFERRAL TRIGGERS

- **IF** Imminent risk of suicide or self-harm is present, **THEN REFER TO** Suicide Module (SUI) immediately.
- **IF** The person is a Child/Adolescent (< 12 years of age), **THEN REFER TO** Child and Adolescent Mental and Behavioural Disorders Module (CMH).
- **IF** Psychotic symptoms (delusions/hallucinations) are present, **THEN** Adapt treatment and **CONSULT A SPECIALIST**.
- **IF** History of Mania is present or symptoms of mania develop during treatment, **THEN REFER TO** Psychoses Module (PSY) and **CONSULT A SPECIALIST**.
- **IF** Concurrent priority MNS (Mental, Neurological, and Substance Use) conditions are suspected, **THEN REFER TO** Master Chart (MC).
- **IF** The patient is a woman of childbearing age considering pregnancy, **THEN CONSULT A SPECIALIST**.

## 3. CLINICAL DECISION LOGIC (IF/THEN PATHS)

**PHASE 1: ASSESSMENT**
- **STEP 1:** **IF** the person has had at least one Core Symptom for `>= 2 weeks`, **THEN** go to STEP 2. **ELSE**, Depression is unlikely -> Go to OTH (Other conditions).
- **STEP 2:** **IF** the person has had several Secondary/Additional Symptoms for `>= 2 weeks`, **THEN** go to STEP 3. **ELSE**, Depression is unlikely -> Go to OTH.
- **STEP 3:** **IF** the person has considerable difficulty with daily functioning (personal, family, social, occupational), **THEN** Consider DEPRESSION and go to STEP 4. **ELSE**, Depression is unlikely -> Go to OTH.
- **STEP 4:** **IF** symptoms are caused by a physical condition (e.g., anaemia, hypothyroidism, substance use), **THEN** manage the physical condition. 
  - **IF** depressive symptoms resolve after physical treatment, **THEN** No treatment needed (Exit).
  - **IF** depressive symptoms remain, **THEN** go to STEP 5.
- **STEP 5:** **IF** the person has a history of Mania (several mania symptoms lasting `>= 1 week`), **THEN** Depressive Episode in Bipolar Disorder is likely -> Go to PROTOCOL 2. **ELSE**, go to STEP 6.
- **STEP 6:** **IF** the person has experienced a major loss (e.g., bereavement) within the last `< 6 months`, **THEN** check for severe symptoms (suicidal ideation, beliefs of worthlessness, psychotic symptoms, or moving/talking slowly). 
  - **IF** severe symptoms are present, **THEN** DEPRESSION is likely -> Go to STEP 7.
  - **IF** severe symptoms are NOT present, **THEN** check for previous history of depression. 
    - **IF** history of depression is YES, **THEN** DEPRESSION is likely -> Go to STEP 7.
    - **IF** history of depression is NO, **THEN** Do not manage for depression -> Go to OTH.
  - **ELSE** (No major loss), DEPRESSION is likely -> Go to STEP 7.
- **STEP 7:** **IF** there are concurrent MNS conditions or imminent suicide risk, **THEN** refer to MC or SUI respectively. **ELSE**, Proceed to PROTOCOL 1 (Depression Management).

**PHASE 2: MANAGEMENT PROTOCOLS & PHARMACOLOGY**
- **STEP 8 (Protocol 2 - Bipolar Depression):** **IF** Depressive episode in bipolar disorder is diagnosed, **THEN** Consult specialist. **NEVER** prescribe antidepressants alone without a mood stabilizer. **IF** mania develops, stop antidepressant immediately.
- **STEP 9 (Protocol 1 - Depression):** **IF** Depression is diagnosed, **THEN** provide psychoeducation, reduce stress, promote daily functioning, consider brief psychological treatments (CBT, IPT), and consider antidepressants.
- **STEP 10 (Medication Selection based on Demographics):**
  - **IF** Adolescent (12+), **THEN** consider Fluoxetine. Ask patient to return weekly for the first 4 weeks to monitor suicidal thoughts.
  - **IF** Woman is pregnant/breastfeeding, **THEN** avoid antidepressants if possible (especially 1st trimester). **IF** needed, use lowest effective dose. **IF** breastfeeding, AVOID long-acting meds (Fluoxetine).
  - **IF** Older Adult, **THEN** avoid Amitriptyline if possible.
  - **IF** Cardiovascular Disease is present, **THEN DO NOT** prescribe Amitriptyline.
  - **IF** Adult has thoughts/plans of suicide, **THEN** SSRIs (Fluoxetine) are first choice. **DO NOT** prescribe TCAs (Amitriptyline) due to fatal overdose risk. Limit supply to 1 week.

**PHASE 3: FOLLOW-UP & MAINTENANCE**
- **STEP 11:** **IF** the patient is improving, **THEN** continue management plan until symptom-free for `9-12 months`. **ELSE**, go to STEP 12.
- **STEP 12:** **IF** the patient is NOT improving:
  - **IF** not on psychological treatment/antidepressants, **THEN** consider starting them.
  - **IF** on antidepressants, check adherence. **IF** adherence is poor, explore reasons. 
  - **IF** adherence is good, check side effects. **IF** side effects are present, weigh benefits. **IF NO** side effects, increase dose (Caution) and follow up in 1-2 weeks.
- **STEP 13 (Mania Check):** **IF** symptoms of mania appear at any contact, **THEN** discontinue antidepressant, treat mania, and consult specialist (Go to PSY).
- **STEP 14 (Tapering):** **IF** the person has been symptom-free for `9-12 months`, **THEN** discuss stopping medication and taper gradually over `>= 4 weeks` while monitoring for recurrence. **ELSE**, continue medication.

## 4. POSSIBLE OUTCOMES

- **Diagnoses:**
  - Depression (Requires Protocol 1)
  - Depressive episode in Bipolar Disorder (Requires Protocol 2)
  - Depression with Psychotic Symptoms
- **Rule-outs / No Diagnosis:**
  - Depression Unlikely (Sub-threshold symptoms / Go to OTH)
  - Normal reaction to major loss / Bereavement (Do not manage for depression)
  - Depressive symptoms secondary to a physical condition (Resolved after physical treatment)
