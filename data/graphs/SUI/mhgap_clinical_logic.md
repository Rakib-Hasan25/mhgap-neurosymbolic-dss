# MODULE: SELF-HARM / SUICIDE

## 1. VARIABLE DICTIONARY
- **Core Symptoms:**
  - Current thoughts or plans of self-harm/suicide
  - Act of self-harm (current or historical)
  - Signs of poisoning or intoxication (e.g., acute pesticide intoxication, cholinergic poisoning, seizures)
  - Bleeding from a self-inflicted wound
  - Loss of consciousness
  - Extreme lethargy
  - Extreme agitation, violence, extreme distress, or lack of communication
- **Secondary Symptoms:**
  - Extreme hopelessness and despair
  - Acute emotional distress
  - Difficulty carrying out usual work, school, domestic, or social activities
  - Repeated self-medication for emotional distress
  - Unexplained physical symptoms
  - Marked distress or repeated help-seeking
- **Concurrent Conditions / Comorbidities:**
  - Priority MNS (Mental, Neurological, Substance use) conditions: Depression, Disorders due to substance use, Child & adolescent mental/behavioral disorders, Psychoses, Epilepsy
  - Chronic pain
- **Duration Thresholds:**
  - Thoughts or plans of self-harm/suicide: in the **past month**
  - Act of self-harm: in the **past year**
  - Time since pesticide poisoning: **< 1 hour** (threshold for administering activated charcoal)
  - Initial intensive follow-up period: **first 2 months**
  - Extended follow-up duration: **2 years**
- **Demographic/Special Flags:**
  - Target demographic: Any person **over 10 years of age**
  - Carer/Family involvement (needs psychosocial support/psychoeducation)

## 2. RED FLAGS & REFERRAL TRIGGERS
- **IF** the person has attempted a medically serious act of self-harm (poisoning, bleeding, loss of consciousness, extreme lethargy), **THEN** immediately execute medical treatment/resuscitation, DO NOT leave alone, and hospitalize if necessary (Protocol 1).
- **IF** there is acute pesticide intoxication AND the facility lacks skills/resources for resuscitation, airway management, intubation, atropine, or diazepam, **THEN REFER/TRANSFER** immediately to a sufficiently equipped health facility.
- **IF** the person has concurrent MNS conditions (Depression, Substance use, Psychoses, Epilepsy, etc.), **THEN REFER TO** the relevant concurrent condition modules for management.
- **IF** the person has severe emotional symptoms requiring clinical management, **THEN REFER TO** the OTH (Other Significant Emotional or Medically Unexplained Complaints) module.
- **IF** during follow-up the person is not improving or risk persists despite management, **THEN REFER TO** a mental health specialist.

## 3. CLINICAL DECISION LOGIC (IF/THEN PATHS)

- **STEP 1: Assessing for Medically Serious Act of Self-Harm**
  - **IF** the person presents with signs of poisoning/intoxication, bleeding from a self-inflicted wound, loss of consciousness, and/or extreme lethargy, **THEN** a medically serious act of self-harm has occurred.
    - Execute **PROTOCOL 1** (Provide secure environment, treat injury/poisoning, initiate pesticide intoxication management if applicable, do not leave alone).
    - **IF** acute pesticide intoxication **AND** facility is equipped, **THEN** manage airway, administer Atropine (for cholinergic signs), administer Diazepam (for seizures).
    - **IF** pesticide poisoning occurred < 1 hour ago, conscious, and consents, **THEN** consider activated charcoal. DO NOT force vomit. DO NOT give oral fluids.
    - Return to Step 2 once the person is medically stable.
  - **ELSE**, proceed directly to STEP 2.

- **STEP 2: Assessing Imminent Risk of Self-Harm/Suicide**
  - **IF** the person has *current* thoughts or plans of self-harm/suicide, **OR** has a history of thoughts/plans in the past month/acts in the past year AND is *currently* extremely agitated, violent, distressed, or lacks communication, **THEN** Imminent Risk is likely.
    - Execute **PROTOCOL 2** (Remove means, secure room, continuous supervision by named staff/family, do not leave alone, psychoeducation, activate psychosocial support, consult specialist).
    - Proceed to STEP 3.
  - **ELSE IF** there are no current thoughts/plans and no severe agitation, BUT there is a history of thoughts/plans in the past month OR an act of self-harm in the past year, **THEN** Imminent risk is unlikely, but Risk persists.
    - Execute **PROTOCOL 3** (Offer and activate psychosocial support, consult specialist, regular contact).
    - Proceed to STEP 3.
  - **ELSE**, Risk of self-harm/suicide is unlikely. Proceed to STEP 3.

- **STEP 3: Assessing Concurrent MNS Conditions**
  - **IF** the person has concurrent MNS conditions (Depression, Substance use, Child & adolescent disorders, Psychoses, Epilepsy), **THEN** manage the concurrent conditions according to relevant modules. Proceed to STEP 4.
  - **ELSE**, Proceed to STEP 4.

- **STEP 4: Assessing Chronic Pain**
  - **IF** the person has chronic pain, **THEN** manage the pain and treat relevant medical conditions. Proceed to STEP 5.
  - **ELSE**, Proceed to STEP 5.

- **STEP 5: Assessing Severe Emotional Symptoms**
  - **IF** the person has difficulty carrying out usual activities, repeated self-medication, unexplained physical symptoms, or marked distress/help-seeking, **THEN** emotional symptoms warrant clinical management. Manage emotional symptoms and go to OTH module.
  - **ELSE**, proceed to Follow-up.

- **STEP 6: Follow-up & Monitoring Routine**
  - **IF** the person is improving, **THEN** decrease contact gradually. Continue follow-up for 2 years (e.g., every 2-4 weeks after the initial 2 months, twice in the second year).
  - **ELSE IF** the person is NOT improving, **THEN** increase intensity or duration of contact and refer to a specialist.
  - *Universal Follow-up Rule:* At every contact, routinely assess for suicidal thoughts and plans. IF risk becomes imminent again, return to STEP 1 (Assessment).

## 4. POSSIBLE OUTCOMES
- **Diagnoses / Risk Classifications:**
  - Medically serious act of self-harm (including acute pesticide intoxication / poisoning / severe injury)
  - Imminent risk of self-harm/suicide
  - Risk of self-harm/suicide (non-imminent)
  - No current risk of self-harm/suicide
- **Rule-outs / Referrals / Concurrent Actions:**
  - Emergency medical transfer (for uncontrolled poisoning/resuscitation)
  - Referral to priority MNS modules (Depression, Psychosis, Substance Use, Epilepsy)
  - Referral to OTH module (Severe emotional symptoms)
  - Management of chronic pain
  - Specialist referral (Mental health specialist due to lack of improvement or high risk)
