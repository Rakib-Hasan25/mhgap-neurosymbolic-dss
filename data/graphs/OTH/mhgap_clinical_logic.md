# MODULE: OTHER SIGNIFICANT MENTAL HEALTH COMPLAINTS

## 1. VARIABLE DICTIONARY
- **Core Symptoms**: Feeling extremely tired, depressed, irritated, anxious, stressed, medically unexplained somatic complaints (e.g., stomach aches, muscle tension, palpitations, aches, pains, gastric upset, headaches).
- **Secondary Symptoms**: Sleep disturbance, sadness, aggression. 
  - *Trauma-specific*: Recurring frightening dreams, flashbacks, intrusive memories, intense fear/horror, deliberate avoidance of reminders, excessive concern/alertness to danger, reacting strongly to loud noises/unexpected movements.
  - *Grief-specific*: Severe preoccupation with or intense longing for the deceased, intense emotional pain, crying, numbness.
- **Duration Thresholds**:
  - `< 1 month`: Expected timeframe for normal trauma reactions.
  - `>= 1 month`: Threshold for suspected Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD).
  - `< 6 months`: Expected timeframe for normal grief.
  - `>= 6 months`: Threshold for suspected Prolonged Grief Disorder.
  - `2 - 4 weeks`: Timeframe for follow-up assessment.
- **Demographic/Special Flags**: 
  - Adult (Target demographic for this module)
  - Child or Adolescent (Exclusion criteria)
  - Exposed to extreme stressors (e.g., physical or sexual violence, major accidents, bereavement, or other major losses)
  - Insistence on further medical investigations

## 2. RED FLAGS & REFERRAL TRIGGERS
- **IF** the patient is a Child or Adolescent, **THEN REFER TO** Child and Adolescent Mental and Behavioural Disorders (CMH) module.
- **IF** there is an imminent risk of self-harm or suicide, **THEN REFER TO** Suicide/Self-harm (SUI) module to assess and manage before continuing.
- **IF** symptoms meet criteria for depression or any other mhGAP priority condition, **THEN REFER TO** the relevant MNS module.
- **IF** a physical cause fully explains the presenting symptoms, **THEN REFER TO** physical health management (manage physical cause and recheck).
- **IF** Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) is suspected (dysfunction $\ge$ 1 month + flashbacks/avoidance/hyper-arousal), **THEN REFER TO** a specialist for further assessment and management.
- **IF** Prolonged Grief Disorder is suspected (dysfunction $\ge$ 6 months + severe preoccupation/intense pain), **THEN REFER TO** a specialist for further assessment and management.

## 3. CLINICAL DECISION LOGIC (IF/THEN PATHS)
- **STEP 1**: **IF** patient is a child or adolescent, **THEN** exit to CMH module. **ELSE** continue.
- **STEP 2**: **IF** there is a physical cause that fully explains the presenting symptoms, **THEN** manage the physical cause and recheck to see if symptoms persist. **ELSE** continue.
- **STEP 3**: **IF** symptoms represent depression or another specific MNS condition, **THEN** exit to relevant module. **ELSE** continue.
- **STEP 4**: **IF** there is imminent risk of suicide, **THEN** assess and manage via SUI module first. **ELSE** continue.
- **STEP 5**: **IF** the person is seeking help to relieve symptoms **OR** having considerable difficulty with daily functioning because of symptoms, **THEN** "Other Significant Mental Health Complaints" are likely (continue to Step 6). **ELSE** no treatment is needed (End Assessment).
- **STEP 6**: **IF** the person has been exposed to extreme stressors (violence, accidents, bereavement, losses), **THEN** proceed to Management Protocol 1 AND 2. **ELSE** proceed to Management Protocol 1 only.
- **STEP 7 (Protocol 1 Management)**: **IF** executing Protocol 1, **THEN**:
  - DO NOT prescribe anti-anxiety or antidepressant medicines.
  - DO NOT give vitamin injections.
  - DO avoid ordering unnecessary labs/investigations unless clear medical indication.
  - DO reduce stress, strengthen social supports, teach relaxation, and explain the link between stress and somatic symptoms.
- **STEP 8 (Protocol 2 - Grief Evaluation)**: **IF** stressor involves a major loss/bereavement:
  - **IF** symptoms cause considerable difficulty with daily functioning for $\ge$ 6 months **AND** include severe preoccupation/intense longing for the deceased **AND** intense emotional pain, **THEN** diagnose suspected Prolonged Grief Disorder and consult a specialist.
  - **ELSE** manage as normal grief (support mourning processes, explain symptoms will likely diminish).
- **STEP 9 (Protocol 2 - Trauma Evaluation)**: **IF** stressor involves a recent potentially traumatic event:
  - **IF** symptoms cause considerable difficulty with daily functioning for $\ge$ 1 month **AND** include flashbacks/intrusive memories **AND** deliberate avoidance **AND** excessive alertness/reactivity, **THEN** diagnose suspected PTSD and consult a specialist.
  - **ELSE** manage as normal reaction to trauma (listen without pressuring to talk, address basic social needs, encourage return to normal activities).
- **STEP 10 (Follow-Up at 2-4 Weeks)**: **IF** the person returns for follow-up:
  - **IF** the person is improving, **THEN** continue the treatment plan and follow up as needed.
  - **ELSE IF** the person is NOT improving **OR** insists on further medical investigations, **THEN** review protocols and consider consulting a specialist.

## 4. POSSIBLE OUTCOMES
- **Diagnoses**:
  - Other Significant Mental Health Complaints (Stress/Somatic)
  - Other Significant Mental Health Complaints in people exposed to extreme stressors (Normal Grief or Normal Trauma Reaction)
  - Suspected Prolonged Grief Disorder
  - Suspected Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)
- **Rule-outs / No Diagnosis**:
  - No treatment needed (Patient is not seeking help and has no daily functioning difficulties)
  - Physical health condition (Symptoms fully explained by a physical medical issue)
  - Depression or other MNS priority condition (Ruled out of this module)
